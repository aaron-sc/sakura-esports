About the Org

Bloom under pressure.

Sakura Eclipse is a multi-title competitive esports organization. We field rosters across five titles, from open-age flagship teams down through dedicated U18 and academy pipelines — every squad training under one banner, one standard.

Our structure is built for development: players move up through Academy, Secondary, and Main tiers as they grow, backed by a staff and community that treats esports as a craft worth taking seriously.

Everything happens in one place — Discord. That's where rosters, scrims, tryouts, and announcements live.
